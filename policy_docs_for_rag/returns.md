# Returns Policy

## Return window
Items can be returned within 7 days of delivery. After 7 days we cannot accept returns.

## Eligible items
Items must be unused, in original packaging, with all accessories and tags. Opened software and hygiene products cannot be returned.

## Damaged or wrong items
If an item arrives damaged, defective, or is not what you ordered, tell us within 7 days of delivery. We will arrange a free pickup and a replacement or full refund.

## How to start a return
Share your order ID and the reason for the return. Pickup is arranged within 2 business days of the return request.

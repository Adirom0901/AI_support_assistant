# Cancellation Policy

## When you can cancel
Orders can be cancelled only while the status is Processing. Once an order is Shipped or Out for Delivery it cannot be cancelled.

## After shipping
If your order has already shipped, you can refuse delivery or return it within 7 days of delivery once it arrives.

## Refund for cancelled orders
Cancelled orders are refunded in full to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

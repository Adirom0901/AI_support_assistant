# Refund Policy

## Refund timeline
Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item reaches our warehouse and passes inspection.

## Refund method
Refunds go to the original payment method. Cash on delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer; we will ask for bank details.

## Partial refunds
Items returned with missing accessories or signs of use may receive a partial refund after inspection.

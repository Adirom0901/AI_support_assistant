# Shipping Policy

## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Remote locations may take up to 8 business days.

## Shipping charges
Shipping is free on orders above Rs. 499. Orders below Rs. 499 have a flat shipping fee of Rs. 49.

## Tracking
A tracking number is shared once the order ships. Orders in Processing do not have a tracking number yet.

## Delivery attempts
The courier makes up to 3 delivery attempts. After that the order is returned to us and refunded.
